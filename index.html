Who We Are

A Trusted Partner For Global Apparel Brands

Lexim Tex Sourcing Ltd. is a full-service apparel sourcing and manufacturing hub headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh. With design teams spanning Bangladesh and Canada, deep-rooted factory relationships, and an experienced merchandising team, we simplify sourcing for brands of every size.

  • Design teams in Bangladesh & Canada

    Trend-driven development close to both production and market.

  • Global fabric & trims sourcing

    Access to verified mills across Bangladesh, China, Taiwan, India and Pakistan.

  • Experienced merchandisers & technical team

    Hands-on management from sampling to shipment.

  • Flexible order quantities

    Lower MOQs designed to support growing and established brands alike.
